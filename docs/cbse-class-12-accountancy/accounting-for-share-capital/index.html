Points students often miss

  • Calls in arrears are deducted from Subscribed Capital in the Balance Sheet note.
  • On forfeiture, Share Capital is debited with the amount called up, and Share Forfeiture is credited with the amount received.
  • Securities premium already received is not reversed on forfeiture.
  • Discount on reissue cannot exceed the forfeited amount on those shares.
  • After reissue, the balance of Share Forfeiture on the reissued shares is transferred to Capital Reserve.
  • In pro rata allotment, excess application money is adjusted first towards allotment, then towards calls.
