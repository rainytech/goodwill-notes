Points students often miss

  • Interest on debentures is always calculated on the face value, never on the issue price.
  • Premium payable on redemption is credited to Premium on Redemption of Debentures Account at the time of issue.
  • Loss on issue of debentures is written off first from Securities Premium, then from the Statement of Profit and Loss.
  • Debentures issued as collateral security are shown as a note under the loan they secure.
  • For debentures issued to a vendor, the number of debentures equals the amount payable ÷ issue price of one debenture.
