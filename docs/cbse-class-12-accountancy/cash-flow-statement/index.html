Worked example: T.S. Grewal Question 36

From the following information, calculate Cash Flow from Financing Activities:

Particulars31st March, 2023 (₹)31st March, 2022 (₹)
Equity Share Capital10,00,0009,00,000
Securities Premium Reserve2,60,0002,50,000
12% Debentures1,00,0001,50,000

Additional information: Interest paid on debentures ₹ 18,000.

Solution: Cash Flow from Financing Activities

ParticularsAmount (₹)
Proceeds from issue of Equity Share Capital (10,00,000 − 9,00,000)1,00,000
Proceeds from Securities Premium Reserve (2,60,000 − 2,50,000)10,000
Redemption of 12% Debentures (1,50,000 − 1,00,000)(50,000)
Interest paid on debentures(18,000)
Cash Flow from Financing Activities42,000

∴ Cash Flow from Financing Activities = ₹ 42,000

Points students often miss

  • Bonus shares are a non-cash transaction, so they bring no cash inflow.
  • Shares or debentures issued on the last day of the year earn dividend or interest only on the opening balance.
  • When preference shares are redeemed at a premium, the cash outflow includes the premium.
  • Interest on debentures is added back under Operating Activities and shown as an outflow under Financing Activities.
  • For a financial enterprise, dividend received is an Operating Activity.
  • When profit after tax is given, add back the provision for tax made to find Net Profit before Tax.
