Points students often miss

  • Percentage change = Absolute change ÷ Previous year's figure × 100.
  • In a Common-Size Statement of Profit and Loss, every item is shown as a percentage of Revenue from Operations.
  • In a Common-Size Balance Sheet, every item is shown as a percentage of Total Assets (or Total Equity and Liabilities).
  • Tax is calculated separately on each year's profit before tax.
  • In missing-figure questions, work backwards from the percentage change to find the amount.
