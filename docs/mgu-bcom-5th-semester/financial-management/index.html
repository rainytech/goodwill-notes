Points students often miss

  • Cost of debt after tax = Interest rate × (1 − Tax rate).
  • Cost of equity by the dividend growth model = Expected dividend ÷ Market price + Growth rate.
  • Weighted average cost of capital uses book value or market value weights.
  • Payback period ignores the time value of money; discounted payback does not.
