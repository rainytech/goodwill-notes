Points students often miss

  • Halsey plan: bonus = 50% of time saved × hourly rate.
  • Rowan plan: bonus = Time saved ÷ Time allowed × Time taken × Hourly rate.
  • Taylor's plan: 83% of the piece rate below standard efficiency, 125% at or above it.
  • Merrick's plan: normal rate up to 83% efficiency, 110% from 83% to 100%, and 120% above 100%.
  • Gantt plan: time wages guaranteed below standard, a bonus at standard, and a high piece rate above it.
