Points students often miss

  • Incidence of tax depends on residential status: resident and ordinarily resident, not ordinarily resident, or non-resident.
  • The new tax regime under section 115BAC is the default regime.
  • Standard deduction from salary is ₹75,000 under the new regime and ₹50,000 under the old regime.
  • HRA exemption is the least of actual HRA, rent paid minus 10% of salary, and 50% (metro) or 40% of salary; it applies only under the old regime.
  • Uncommuted pension is fully taxable as salary.
