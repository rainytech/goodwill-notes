Points students often miss

  • Realisation Account records the sale of assets and the payment of outside liabilities.
  • Profit or loss on realisation is shared in the old profit sharing ratio.
  • An asset taken over by a partner is debited to that partner's Capital Account.
  • A partner's loan is paid through the Cash or Bank Account, not through the Realisation Account.
  • Accumulated profits and reserves go to the partners' capital accounts in the old ratio.
