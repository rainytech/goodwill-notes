Points students often miss

  • Sacrificing ratio = old ratio − new ratio.
  • Goodwill brought by the new partner is credited to the old partners in the sacrificing ratio.
  • Revaluation profit or loss belongs to the old partners in the old ratio.
  • To find proportionate capital, start from the new partner's capital and his share of profit.
  • Accumulated reserves and profits go to the old partners before admission.
