Points students often miss

  • Gaining ratio = new ratio − old ratio.
  • The retiring partner's share of goodwill is debited to the gaining partners in the gaining ratio.
  • On death, profit up to the date of death is found on time basis or turnover basis.
  • The deceased partner's share of profit is debited to Profit and Loss Suspense Account.
  • The amount due to a retiring partner can be transferred to his Loan Account.
