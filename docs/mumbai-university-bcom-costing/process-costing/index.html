Points students often miss

  • The scrap value of normal loss is credited to the Process Account.
  • Cost per unit = (Total cost − Scrap value of normal loss) ÷ (Input − Normal loss).
  • Abnormal loss and abnormal gain are valued at that cost per unit.
  • Output is transferred to the next process at cost.
