Points students often miss

  • Notional profit = Work certified + Work not certified − Cost of work to date.
  • Below 25% completion, no profit is taken to the Profit and Loss Account.
  • From 25% to 50% completion: one-third of notional profit × Cash received ÷ Work certified.
  • At 50% or more: two-thirds of notional profit × Cash received ÷ Work certified.
  • Retention money is the part of work certified not yet paid by the contractee.
