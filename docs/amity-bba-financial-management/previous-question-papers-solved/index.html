Points students often miss

  • Payback period is the time taken to recover the initial investment from cash inflows.
  • For the part year: unrecovered amount ÷ cash inflow of the next year.
  • Discounted payback uses the present value of each year's cash inflow.
  • Compound value = Principal × (1 + r) raised to the power n.
  • EOQ = square root of (2 × Annual demand × Ordering cost ÷ Carrying cost per unit).
