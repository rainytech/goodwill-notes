Points students often miss

  • Closing stock given inside the Trial Balance goes only to the Balance Sheet.
  • Outstanding expenses are added to the expense and shown as a liability.
  • Prepaid expenses are deducted and shown as an asset.
  • Further bad debts are deducted before calculating the new provision.
  • Interest on capital is an expense; interest on drawings is an income.
