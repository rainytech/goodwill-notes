Points students often miss

  • Rent is usually apportioned on floor area and advertisement on sales.
  • Expenses that cannot be apportioned go to the General Profit and Loss Account.
  • Inter-departmental transfers may carry a profit loading.
  • Unrealised profit on closing stock from transfers is eliminated through a stock reserve.
  • Departmental Trading and Profit and Loss Accounts are prepared in columnar form.
