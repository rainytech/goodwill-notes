Points students often miss

  • Under the Debtors System, the Branch Account shows the profit or loss of the branch.
  • When goods are sent at invoice price, the loading is removed through a stock reserve or Branch Adjustment Account.
  • If invoice price is cost plus 25%, the loading is one-fifth of the invoice price.
  • Cash sales and collections from branch debtors remitted to head office are credited to the Branch Account.
  • Abnormal loss is valued at cost and taken to the Profit and Loss Account.
