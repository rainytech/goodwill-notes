Points students often miss

  • Hire purchase price = cash price + total interest.
  • Interest is calculated on the cash price still outstanding.
  • The hire purchaser records the asset at the full cash price and charges depreciation on it.
  • In complete repossession, the vendor takes back all the goods and the buyer closes the asset account.
  • In partial repossession, the goods taken back are valued at an agreed depreciated value.
