Points students often miss

  • Prepare a Memorandum Trading Account to estimate the stock on the date of fire.
  • Leave abnormal items out of the Memorandum Trading Account.
  • Average clause: Claim = Loss × Policy Value ÷ Value of Stock on the date of fire.
  • The average clause applies only when the stock is under-insured.
  • Loss of profit is based on the short sales during the indemnity period.
