Points students often miss

  • Minimum rent is payable even when the royalty earned is lower.
  • Short workings arise when royalty is less than the minimum rent.
  • Short workings can be recouped only within the period the agreement allows.
  • Short workings that can no longer be recouped are written off to the Profit and Loss Account.
