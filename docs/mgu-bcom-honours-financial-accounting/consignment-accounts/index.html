Points students often miss

  • A consignment is not a sale; ownership stays with the consignor.
  • Unsold stock is valued at cost plus a proportionate share of non-recurring expenses.
  • Normal loss is absorbed into the cost of the remaining goods.
  • Abnormal loss is valued at cost and credited to the Consignment Account.
  • With del credere commission, the consignee bears the bad debts.
